Freedom in Celebration

Holidays and freedom — Let it be

⏱️ 30-minute activity (A2 - Roots)
1

Opening: Why Holidays?

3 min

Today is a holiday. Why do we celebrate? Is it just a day off work? Or is it something more important?

In the Bible, Paul tells his friends (the Colossians) about real freedom. He says: Don't worry about eating this food or that food. Don't worry about special days. The most important thing is Christ.

CEFR Level: A2 (Roots)
Simple vocabulary, more support
2

Listen & Learn

7 min

Let's LISTEN to the passage. You don't need to understand every word. Just listen and feel the message.

Listen to the Bible verse
Therefore do not let anyone judge you by what you eat or drink, or with regard to a religious festival, a New Moon celebration or a Sabbath day. These are a shadow of the things that were to come; the reality, however, is found in Christ.
Colossians 2:16-17 (NIV)

📌 What is this verse about? Paul says: Don't let people tell you what to eat. Don't worry about holidays. The important thing is Christ.

3

Important Words

6 min

These words help us understand the verse. Let's learn them one by one.

judge
to say if something is good or bad
português: julgar
freedom
not controlled by others; free
português: liberdade
festival
a celebration or party
português: festival
Christ
Jesus, the center of Christian faith
português: Cristo
🔍 Simple Grammar: "Do not let" = "Don't allow". Example: "Do not let anyone judge you" = "Don't let people judge you."
4

Practice

10 min

Activity 1: Multiple Choice

1. What should we NOT worry about?
A) Jesus (Christ)
B) What we eat or drink
C) God
2. Paul says: "___________ do not let anyone judge you."
A) Therefore
B) Because
C) However

Activity 2: True or False

The verse says: "Festivals and holidays are very important."
5

Final Message

4 min
"Do not let anyone judge you..."

Paul's message is simple: You don't need to follow all the rules about food or holidays. Christ is what is real and important. Freedom means being free from what others think you should do.

What you learned today:

  • ✅ Four important words: judge, freedom, festival, Christ
  • ✅ The main idea: Christ is more important than holidays and rules
  • ✅ The simple grammar: "Do not let" = "Don't allow"
  • ✅ Real freedom comes from Christ, not from rules
Great job! You listened, learned, and practiced. This is real learning.
