Freedom in Celebration

Exploring holidays, festivals & spiritual freedom

⏱️ 30-minute activity
1

Opening: The Question

3 min

Today is a holiday. But what makes a celebration truly meaningful? Is it the day off from work? The family time? Or something deeper?

In today's passage, Paul writes to the Colossians about exactly this: the difference between observing external rules and finding freedom in Christ.

CEFR Level: Growth (B1) - Flourish (B2)
Focus: Reading, Discussion, Reflection
2

Listen & Absorb

7 min

First, let's LISTEN to the passage. Pay attention to the rhythm, the key words you recognize, and the overall meaning.

Listen to Colossians 2:16-17 (NIV)

📌 Tip: You won't understand every word—that's completely normal! Listen for familiar words: "judge", "eat", "drink", "festival", "Sabbath", "shadow", "Christ".

Therefore do not let anyone judge you by what you eat or drink, or with regard to a religious festival, a New Moon celebration or a Sabbath day. These are a shadow of the things that were to come; the reality, however, is found in Christ.
Colossians 2:16-17 (NIV)
3

Read & Discover Vocabulary

6 min

Now let's READ the passage and discover the key vocabulary. Notice how these words appear IN CONTEXT—not in isolation.

festival
A celebration or holiday (religious or cultural)
shadow
A faint image; something not real or permanent
judge
To form an opinion or pass judgment on someone
Sabbath
A day of rest (Saturday in Jewish tradition)
reality
What is actually real or true
New Moon
A monthly celebration in Jewish tradition
🔍 Grammar Discovery: Notice the phrase "do not let anyone judge you." This is an imperative (command). Paul is saying: "Refuse to be judged." The structure is: "Do not + let + anyone + verb."
4

Interactive Exploration

10 min

Activity A: Fill in the Missing Words

1. Do not let anyone __________ you by what you eat or drink.
2. These things are a __________ of the things that were to come.
3. The __________, however, is found in Christ.

Activity B: Discussion Prompt

💬 What is the difference between a "shadow" and "reality" when it comes to celebrating holidays? How does Christ become the "reality"?
5

Closing: The Takeaway

4 min
"Therefore do not let anyone judge you..."

Paul's invitation to freedom is radical: Stop living by external rules that others impose. Find the real thing—Christ—not the shadow. When you celebrate, celebrate because you're connected to something real and eternal.

What you learned today:

  • ✅ Vocabulary in context: festival, shadow, reality, judge, Sabbath
  • ✅ A message of spiritual freedom: External rules are shadows; Christ is the reality
  • ✅ Cultural insight: Understanding Jewish festivals through a Christian lens
  • ✅ Personal reflection: Questioning what truly matters in celebration
