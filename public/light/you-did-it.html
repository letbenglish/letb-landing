Wow. Look how far we’ve come!

Six weeks ago, we started in the dark... and God said, “Let there be light!”

Since then, we looked at the birds. We learned that God made you, and that He loves you. We named animals (and maybe invented a few new ones!). We played hide-and-seek and found out that God always looks for us. And we sailed with Noah until a little dove came back with a leaf.

Do you remember what that little leaf meant? A new beginning.

That’s exactly where your family is right now.

Thirty little moments around the table. Thirty stamps. Six letters.

One family that opened the Bible together.
